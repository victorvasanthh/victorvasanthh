## About

I work on embedded systems where firmware, hardware, and machine learning have to
meet in the same device. My hands-on work covers embedded firmware in C, board
bring-up and hardware debugging, functional testing and hardware validation, PCB
integration, and device driver development on STM32, ESP32, TI Tiva-C, and
Raspberry Pi platforms.

On the digital design side, I develop and verify hardware architectures in Verilog
on the Xilinx Zynq-7010, targeting accelerators that have to close timing inside a
fixed on-chip resource budget. I also deploy and characterize deep learning models
for edge inference on NVIDIA Jetson Nano and Raspberry Pi, where latency, memory,
and power constraints decide what is actually deployable.

Most of what I build is system-level: sensor to firmware to interface to host, or
model to accelerator to board, validated end to end rather than in isolation.

## Featured Engineering Projects

### IoT-Based Renewable Energy Microgrid
`ESP32` · `Embedded C` · `Blynk`

Designed and validated an ESP32-based microgrid integrating solar panels, battery
storage, relay modules, and driver ICs. Implemented the control and acquisition
firmware in Embedded C, interfaced the relay and driver circuitry to the
microcontroller, and built real-time monitoring over the Blynk IoT platform.
Verified the integrated system at the board level rather than per subsystem.

### UART-Based Distance Sensing System
`STM32` · `TI Tiva-C` · `UART`

Developed an ultrasonic distance measurement system on STM32 and TI Tiva-C
targets. Implemented the sensor timing and acquisition firmware, transported
measurements to the host over UART in real time, and functionally validated the
full path from echo capture to reported distance.

### FPGA-Accelerated KNN Architecture
`Xilinx Zynq` · `Verilog` · `RTL Verification`

Designed a hardware K-nearest-neighbours accelerator in Verilog and verified it on
a Xilinx Zynq FPGA. Structured the datapath for parallel distance computation
within the available on-chip resources, and evaluated the design against the
software baseline for both execution performance and energy efficiency.

### Real-Time FPGA Pipeline for Agricultural AI
`FPGA` · `Real-Time Inference` · `Computer Vision`

Developed and validated a real-time embedded inference pipeline for crop disease
detection on FPGA. Partitioned the pipeline to meet real-time timing requirements
under practical logic and memory constraints, and verified behaviour on hardware
against the reference implementation.

### Hybrid Deep Learning for Blood Cell Classification
`Jetson Nano` · `ResNet-18` · `MobileNetV2`

Deployed and tested ResNet-18 and MobileNetV2 models on NVIDIA Jetson Nano for
blood cell classification at the edge. Compared the two backbones on the target
hardware to characterize the accuracy-versus-inference-cost trade-off under
embedded resource limits.
